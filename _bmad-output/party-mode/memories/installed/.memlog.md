- (outcome) Key strategic pivot: Master wanted an agent that joins live phone calls and warns during the conversation. Room found Google already ships this (Pixel 9+, Gemini Nano, on-device, real-time) and that iOS/Android both structurally block third-party apps from live call-audio access (policy-level, not just technical). Decision: Master chose to CEDE the live-call-audio layer to OS vendors and focus scam-guard on the layers Google doesn't cover — install-level, message-level, account-level signals, cross-signal correlation, cross-platform/older-device coverage, and the family/concierge layers.
