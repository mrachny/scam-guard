Partner & Investor Brief · Scam-Guard · v0.1 · 2026-09-29

The fraud-loss sensor already missing from your stack.

Scam-Guard is a cross-signal scam-detection layer — install, message, and account activity, correlated across a person's own devices — built to plug into the fraud infrastructure banks, insurers, and telecoms already run, not replace it.

The problem, in numbers

$352M+FBI-reported AI-voice-scam losses targeting adults 60+, 2025

Every one of these losses is currently detected — if at all — after the money moves. Scam-Guard's install/message/account correlation is built to catch the pattern in the 90-minute window while it's still forming, before the transaction, not after.

What it is

Cross-signal correlation

Any single signal — a remote-access install, an urgent SMS, a password reset — is weak alone. Two or three, clustered within an hour, is a composite incident. Same model enterprise SIEM/XDR already runs, applied to a household.

Deliberately not competing with Google

Live call-audio detection is ceded to OS vendors (Pixel/Gemini Nano already ships it, and platform policy blocks third parties from that layer anyway). Scam-Guard owns the layers Google doesn't: install, message, account, and cross-device correlation.

Standards-based, not bespoke

FIDO2/WebAuthn for device legitimacy, CVSS/SSVC-derived severity scoring, MISP/STIX/TAXII for confirmed-pattern distribution. Integrates with infrastructure your security team already recognizes.

Confirmation before broadcast

A pattern is a "Rumor" until independent, trust-weighted corroboration confirms it — severity-scaled, so a false positive can't propagate at the same speed as a true one.

Why a bank, insurer, or telecom partners instead of builds

You already carry the loss when this fails — reimbursement liability, fraud-insurance payouts, or spoofed-call regulatory exposure. Scam-Guard is built to sit in front of that loss as a bundled, white-label benefit on an existing account, rather than asking your customer to discover and trust a new brand mid-crisis.

The ask: a pilot integration — Scam-Guard's confirmed-incident feed wired into your existing fraud desk, bundled as a no-cost benefit for a defined customer segment, measured against your current fraud-loss baseline.
